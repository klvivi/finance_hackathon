Example proposal

Bookkeeping Agent

An agent that closes the books every month. You upload the bank statement file, and it categorizes every transaction, reconciles against the statement, and hands the accountant a finished workbook. You only review what it flags.

All names and amounts on this page are fictional examples
How it works

The monthly close in five steps

  1. Upload the statement

    You upload the month's CSV export and type the closing balance.

  2. Categorize

    Sorts each item: income, rent, software, contractors.

  3. Reconcile

    Checks opening balance + inflows − outflows against the statement.

  4. You review

    Only items missing a receipt or needing a decision.

  5. Deliver

    Produces the monthly workbook for the accountant.

Security

What it can do and what it never does

Can

  • Read the statement file you upload
  • Suggest categories and flag open items
  • Produce the workbook and monthly summary

Never

  • Move money, pay, or transfer
  • Log into the bank or see any key
  • Send anything to the accountant without approval
